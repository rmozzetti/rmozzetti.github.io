AI-Powered Game Reverse Engineering and Decompilation

Home History How? What happens now?

AI-assisted game development has advanced quickly, and it is an exciting time as both a developer as well as a user to see how this will impact development and gaming alike. This isn't without it's share of issues though, as the use of AI to replicate existing IPs does come with legal concerns. It appears Anthropic has already began to crack down on that use, as they have restricted Claude Code from recreating games using their AI agent. Despite that, it's interesting to see how AI will continue to innovate, and impact game development in the future.
