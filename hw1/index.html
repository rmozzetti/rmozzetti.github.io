AI-Powered Game Reverse Engineering and Decompilation

Home History How? What happens now?

There have been many video examples floating around of people using AI agents such as Claude to reverse engineer existing video games, and combine them with each other. What would normally take extensive amounts of time can be done at a rapid pace thanks to AI. Its use spans past just adding Minecraft to Dark Souls, as it allows for older games to be decompiled which helps developers reconstruct older games and port them, preserving older titles that are harder to play now. I hope to explain the background of what is happening, how it works, and what happens now.
